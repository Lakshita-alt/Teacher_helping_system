# Platform Demonstration Guide: Teacher Helping System

This guide outlines the narrative flow, key features, and script for a high-impact video demonstration of the **Learning Pattern Analysis & Guidance Framework**.

## Demonstration Flow

### Phase 1: The Administrative "Macro" View
**Location:** `/admin/school`
- **Action:** Show the metric grid (Active Sections: 14).
- **Dialogue:** *"Welcome to the Teacher Helping System. We start at the institutional level. Administrators can monitor 14 unique sections, tracking 6,600+ students through real-time behavioral archetypes, not just static grades."*
- **Action:** Scroll through the Class Overviews. Click one to show the dynamic persona distribution.

### Phase 2: Targeted Faculty Assignment
**Location:** `/admin/assign`
- **Action:** Select a faculty member and Class N. Click "Authorize Assignment".
- **Dialogue:** *"Scale is managed by linking specialized faculty to specific student batches. Here, we authorize a new assignment, synchronizing the teacher's dashboard with the specific needs of Class N."*

### Phase 3: The Teacher's "Priority Inbox"
**Location:** `/home`
- **Action:** Log in as a teacher. Point to the **Priority Inbox**.
- **Dialogue:** *"As a teacher, my morning starts with the Priority Inbox. The system doesn't just show me risk—it shows me 'Predictive Drift.' It flags students whose participation velocity is dropping before they actually fail a test."*
- **Action:** Open the **Daily Pulse Logger**. Mark a few students as 'Active' or 'Passive'. Click Sync.
- **Dialogue:** *"I can record the 'Micro-Signals' of today's classroom energy in seconds. This real-time qualitative data feeds the entire behavioral model."*

### Phase 4: Understanding Archetypes (The Lexicon)
**Location:** `/home/personas`
- **Action:** Scroll through the cards. Highlight "Motivated but Under-Resourced".
- **Dialogue:** *"To move away from black-box labels, our Persona Lexicon provides qualitative depth. Teachers can understand the specific 'Pedagogical Hurdles' and 'Recommended Scaffolding' for each student archetype."*

### Phase 5: Cluster-Based Intervention (Efficiency at Scale)
**Location:** `/home/class`
- **Action:** Go to the **Cluster Strategy Engine**. Select a persona group and click "Generate Batch Plan".
- **Dialogue:** *"In a large class, I can't manage 30 individual plans. Our Cluster Strategy Engine groups students by persona, allowing me to generate one high-level pedagogical shift that benefits the entire group simultaneously."*

### Phase 6: Individual Deep-Dive & Social Scaffolding
**Location:** `/home/student`
- **Action:** Select a High-Risk student. Show the **Longitudinal Evolution** timeline.
- **Dialogue:** *"For critical cases, we deep-dive. We see the 'Behavioral Narrative'—how a student moved from Active to Fragmented over 4 months. More importantly, the system suggests 'Social Scaffolding'—matching this student with a specific peer mentor in the same class to catalyze growth."*
- **Action:** Show the **Manual Adjustment** panel.
- **Dialogue:** *"Finally, we respect the teacher's intuition. I can manually override or snooze alerts, ensuring the AI remains a fair and collaborative partner in the classroom."*

---

## Key Innovation Highlights
1. **Predictive Drift:** Detecting disengagement via participation velocity.
2. **Qualitative Lexicon:** Transforming data labels into pedagogical profiles.
3. **Instructional Scaffolding:** AI-generated lesson parameters tailored to class personas.
4. **Social Scaffolding:** Peer-mentorship matching based on behavioral synergy.
5. **Human-in-the-loop:** Manual risk recalibration for educator oversight.
